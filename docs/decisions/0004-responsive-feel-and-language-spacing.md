# 0004 Responsive feel, focus by expansion, language spacing

Purpose: the handbook feels as immediate as Story Forge everywhere, no control ever ignores or escapes the pointer, and Chinese reads as comfortably as English.

Status: established on 2026-10-30. Application state: applied to `src/` and `ui-handbook.html`.

## Decisions

1. **Hover timing returns to Story Forge; a hovered component does not move.** Serves: the feel the owner tuned. The .15s intent delay stays. The felt wait came from the 500ms attention lock, which Story Forge applies between two distant modules and which this handbook had applied between adjacent cards, so moving from one card to the next took 500ms. The lock now applies only with `data-lock`. Example: `<div class="sf" data-scope="sf" data-lock>`. Hover adds only a shadow, in .2s through `--lift`, with no displacement; this departs from Story Forge `.cp-sec`, which also moves 1px, because the owner found the movement itself uncomfortable.
2. **Hover dims others; only an expanded component keeps them dim.** Serves: one consistent rule with no click needed. Moving the pointer onto any component dims the others on that surface after .15s, whether or not it is clicked, and they recover .15s after it leaves, the same delay as Story Forge. `data-hold` marks components whose click expands content or takes space: the dashboard Income card (decision 0005), nodes, blueprint, maps, agent crew and command list. Those keep others dim after the pointer leaves, until a click outside.
3. **Every card on the page takes part.** Narrowed by 0005 decision 15: display-only components only gain a shadow. Serves: no sample left out. Scopes no longer opt out of dimming, apart from the workspace, which does its own dimming, and single-component surfaces. Explanatory cards are declared as components when the page starts. Moving between adjacent components switches at once, because the others are already dim, and they do not flicker. The top bar does not dim the page, so a theme can be judged while it is being chosen.
4. **Navigation follows the rules.** Serves: chrome and content stop competing. Page gutters grew from 40 to 56px so the rail's ticks have their own column; at 1440 wide the rail ends at 46px and content starts at 115px. On hover the rail is frosted glass and `#page` dims to .3; on leave it recovers. The phone navigation does the same while open.
5. **One indicator for every selector.** Serves: unified selection. `src/ux.js` follows the selected option frame by frame, so it stays correct while an option resizes. Theme dots keep their positions when selected: their shifting caused the next click to land on the wrong dot. The current theme card stays wider, as it was; an outline tried for it was removed because the owner rejected it and rule 4 forbids it. The colour bar on theme cards shows `sunk`, `ground`, `card`, `sel`, `ink2`, `ink` and `signal`, none of which equals the card's own colour, so it has no gaps and needs no line.
6. **Clicks survive page transitions.** Serves: no ignored click. During a View Transition the browser delivers every click to `<html>`; `pointer-events:none` on `::view-transition` did not change this in the tested browser. `UX.transition` ends the transition on press and replays the click on the real element on release. Measured with real clicks: three theme dots clicked in quick succession changed the theme at 437, 698 and 1197ms.
7. **Language change cross-fades and pins scroll.** Serves: a switch fit for a real site. The first fully visible element keeps its screen position: in the test, the element stayed at y=432 while the page above it changed height by 813px.
8. **Chinese vertical spacing is one token.** Serves: Chinese no longer feels cramped. `--y` is 1.3 in Chinese and 1 in English, and vertical paddings and margins multiply it. Example: `.card{padding:calc(12px*var(--y)) 14px}` gives 15.6px in Chinese and 12px in English. Labels, tags and buttons have a fixed line height, so a label no longer grows taller in Chinese.
9. **Press and hold is fixed at its cause.** Serves: a control that stays under the finger. `.btn:active{transform:scale(.95)}` replaced the button's centring `transform:translateX(-50%)`, so it jumped sideways on press and lost the pointer. Centring now uses `translate`, and the hold captures the pointer.
10. **The agent panel no longer overflows.** Serves: panels keep their bounds. A full log grew the grid row past its 360px height, so the queue overlapped the controls and the next heading. The row is now `minmax(0,1fr)`.
11. **Clipped content fades.** Serves: no hard edges. The collapsed ledger, agent log and phone panel use `mask-image`.
12. **Row actions sit cleanly on their row.** Serves: a hover action that reads as part of the row. The action now covers the row's right end to its full height and fades from the row's own colour (`--row-bg`), so an unread dot no longer shows beside it. A ghost button no longer brightens on hover: `.btn:hover{filter:brightness(1.12)}` was turning its grey background white.

## Acceptance

Checked by script and real pointer in the browser pane on 2026-10-30, at 1440 wide unless noted: a card is not in hover at 100ms and is at 170ms, and at that moment the other cards are dim without any click; moving to the next card switches at once and the others stay dim; 170ms after leaving, the others have recovered; a clicked card that expands nothing behaves the same; a clicked component that expands, now the dashboard Income card, keeps others dim after the pointer leaves, until a click outside; the workspace chat is not admitted at 250ms and is at 570ms; a hovered card's `translate` is `none`; the page opacity is .3 with the rail hovered and 1 after leaving; a full agent log leaves 13px between the queue and the controls; a press transform does not move the hold button's centre (0px); no console errors; no page-level overflow at 375 wide in either language.

Not checked: a real phone, a real long press with a physical mouse (the hold flow was exercised by keyboard, and the press position by measurement), and every section in every theme.

## Owner record

2026-10-30: 「是鼠标hover的时候，其他组件就淡化，而不是点击才淡化。没有展开的组件，点击不点击，其他组件都是淡化，因为他自己本身没有其他的变化」 Gloss: "When the mouse hovers, the other components dim; dimming does not wait for a click. For a component that has not expanded, the others dim whether or not it is clicked, because it has no other change of its own." 「我知道为什么抬起那么不舒服了，不要移动，只显式阴影」 Gloss: "I know why the lift felt so uncomfortable: do not move it, only show a shadow." The quotations record the direction; the decisions above are the operative statement.

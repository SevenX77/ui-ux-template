# 0005 Push detail in place and live reorder

Purpose: a user can open detail or rearrange items without leaving the surface they are reading, and sees the result while acting, not after it.

Status: established on 2026-10-30. Application state: applied to `src/` and `ui-handbook.html`.

## Decisions

1. **Detail opens in place and pushes its neighbours aside; the board keeps its size.** Serves: reading the detail together with the rest of the board. Clicking the Income card grows it from 492 to 816 wide at 1440, and the other cards move into a right-hand column. The board stays 543 high in Chinese and 480 in English, so nothing below it moves. This is the Push of rule 9, because both are read together.
2. **The whole layout is one animated property set, so it can be interrupted.** Serves: no jump when the user changes their mind. On desktop every card on the board is absolutely placed with `left`, `top`, `width` and `height` from `--l`, `--t`, `--wd` and `--ht`, which are transitioned in .52s. Opening and closing mid-way continues from the current width: measured 713, then 592, then 816.
3. **The expanded content has a fixed width during the push.** Serves: text that does not reflow frame by frame. `.inc-more` is `calc(68cqw - 32px)` from the start; the card reveals it as it grows.
4. **Pushed cards stay usable.** Serves: the board remains a board. The other dashboard cards are declared `data-nofocus data-keep`: hovering one gives it a shadow and takes it out of the dim, and clicking one does not close Income. A click elsewhere, the close button or Esc closes it, and the range returns to 30 days.
5. **Only Income holds on the dashboard.** Serves: rule 7. The surface no longer has `data-hold`, because the other cards no longer expand on click.
6. **The phone form uses flow, not a pushed column.** Serves: rule 18. At 375 wide the cards stack, and Income opens downward: the board grows from 1163 to 1695 high with no horizontal overflow.
7. **A dragged item takes its place while moving.** Serves: watching and adjusting in real time. `UX.sortable` leaves a recessed slot in the list and floats a copy of the card under the pointer. The slot moves to the position nearest the card as soon as the card reaches it, the other cards make way through `UX.flip`, and the column counts update. Releasing only drops the card into the slot.
8. **Positions are computed as if the slot were absent.** Serves: an order that does not flicker. A position's top does not depend on where the slot currently is, so the nearest position is a function of the card's location alone. Measured: 30 one-pixel moves around a position caused 0 reorders, and the same held for items of different heights.
9. **The pointer is captured on the list, and the browser's own drag is no longer used.** Serves: one consistent feel. Browser drag draws its own translucent image of the card, including its hover tools, and does not work on touch. With capture on the list, no other component reacts during the drag and `:hover` stays on the list.
10. **One mechanism for every sortable list.** Serves: rule 6, no sample writes its own version. The kanban and the node configuration list both call `UX.sortable`. The configuration list reordered live before, but without animation.
11. **Dragging keeps what browser drag gave.** Serves: nothing lost in the change. Near the top or bottom of the window the page scrolls (368px in 400ms at 10px from the bottom; it stops when the pointer moves away). Esc returns the card to its origin. Touch starts after a 180ms hold and a finger that moves first scrolls the page.
12. **Interrupted FLIP continues from what is seen.** Serves: no jump when positions change again before the previous animation ends. `UX.flip` cancels its previous animation before measuring the new layout.

13. **A visible state ends with what started it.** Serves: nothing left behind after the user moved on. A blueprint line vanishing on pointer leave was one case of a wider class, so every sample was audited by reading the code, and each finding was checked against the source before it was fixed. The causes, each with one example:
    - **Mouse focus held a state open.** `:focus-within` keeps a tool visible after a click on it, until the next click anywhere. Tools, row actions, the canvas filter rail and parameter values now use `:has(:focus-visible)`, which a mouse click on a button does not match and a text field still does.
    - **A press moved the target before release.** Clicking Run with a node selected closed the panel on press, so the button slid away and the click was lost. Run is now `data-keep`.
    - **Two owners for one state.** The command list followed DOM focus while dimming followed UX focus, so Esc left the list open and others undimmed. UX focus now follows the input's focus.
    - **Selection cleared in part.** Workspace selections share one `clearSel`, so a clip outline, text highlight and context pill always leave together.
    - **Rebuilt or late content ignored current state.** Wires rebuilt during a run went dark and now keep a lit set; new agent log lines ignored the crew filter; timers from before a reset wrote old lines into the cleared log.
    - **Text fixed in one language.** The AI stream, phone navigation label, calendar weekdays, file search and edited notes now follow the current language.
    - **Replayed click without its release.** The click replay after a page transition sent a press with no release, which left the canvas following the pointer. It now sends both, with a valid pointer id.
14. **The stage scrolls at medium widths.** Serves: no node out of reach. Between 901 and 1192 wide, the open panel leaves the stage narrower than the nodes (599 against 805 at 1000 wide). The stage now scrolls sideways and fades at its right edge. Below 600 of stage width the phone layout now applies only on a phone-width window, so opening the panel no longer moves the nodes.

15. **A display-only component does not dim others.** Serves: attention only moves where something can happen. This narrows 0004 decision 3, under which every card dimmed the rest. Dimming tells the user which component is about to respond, and a component with nothing to trigger has nothing to announce, so it only gains a shadow and takes no focus. Of the 144 components, 73 hold no control, and 21 of those change in response to the pointer or a click and declare `data-live`: nodes, blueprint groups, crew, theme cards, motion demos and script-driven samples. The other 52 are display-only, such as the four term cards.
16. **Calendar hover no longer shakes.** Serves: a stable surface while previewing. There were two causes. The task list went between one and two lines, so the card and everything around it changed height; it now reserves two lines. And each move between days reverted to the selected day and only then, .15s later, showed the next, so the list flashed. `UX.intent` now defers the revert by .15s and switches directly when the pointer reaches a neighbouring item, which also steadies the role map and fan lists that use it.

## Acceptance

Checked in the browser pane on 2026-10-30 at 1440 wide unless noted.

- Display-only and calendar, by script unless noted: the calendar card stayed 394 high across days with 0, 1 and 2 tasks; the list went from one previewed day to the next with no flash back, and returned to the selected day .15s after leaving; a fast pass across days changed nothing. A term card under a real pointer was in hover with the other three at rest and opacity 1, and by script it took no focus when pressed. The calendar was not moved across with a real pointer, because the test pane was hidden. Hovering a spending card dimmed nothing, while hovering Income dimmed 5; moving from Income to the spending card undimmed them at once, and moving back took the .15s intent delay. Nodes and theme cards still dim others.

- State audit, with a real pointer: with a node selected, one click on Run started the run and kept the panel open; after a click on the stream card's regenerate tool, the tool was hidden 0.6s after the pointer left; the command list opened on click with others dim, and Esc closed it and undimmed them; a click on the card's label did nothing; a row click ran the command; deleting all typed text in the chat left `<br>` before the fix.
- State audit, by script: a clip's selection cleared with the panel's close button; text and clip selections replaced each other; mode switches cleared the selection and pill; the timeline menu closed when the timeline went to rest; wires stayed lit (3 before and after selecting a node mid-run); discard restored the switch; a finished backup stayed at 214 / 214; the heatmap label reset; Esc with the pointer on a blueprint group kept its 8 preview lines, and leaving cleared them; a window return did not refocus; English weekdays read MTWTFSS and returned to Chinese; `plan` found Release-plan.pdf in English; an edited note survived a switch to Chinese; at 1000 wide the nodes kept their positions when the panel opened and closed, Video was reachable by scrolling, and the panel's controls were out of tab order when closed; notes mode left the dashboard at 543 high; no overflow at 375 in either language.

- Income: range 7 shows $7,480 and the previous $7,180; the close button, Esc and an outside click all close it; closing resets to $32,023; clicking a side card leaves it open; others stay dim after the pointer leaves and a hovered side card leaves the dim; no overflow in English (side cards 332 by 332); Prism screenshot reads correctly.
- Kanban, with synthetic pointer events: nothing starts below 4px; the floating card starts exactly on the original (0px offset); within a column the order changes while moving (`确认片头字体` moved from first to last, count 3); moving to another column puts it there at once, counts 2 and 2, and that column gains the drop outline while the others dim; a column emptied by the drag shows 0 and accepts the card back; after release the floating card and slot are gone; Esc restored the original order; the hover tool `打开` still fires its click.
- Kanban, with a real pointer: `Trailer C` dragged to the Done column landed between `剧本第三稿` and `选角`, counts 1 and 3.
- Configuration list: dragging by the grip reordered it live, and the floating copy showed the current slider value (70).
- No console errors.

Not checked: a real phone and real touch (the 180ms hold and the blocking of scroll while dragging), a real pointer on the configuration list, the hover state of other components during a real drag (the tool drags without a pause, so decision 9's capture was read from the code and from the state after release), reduced motion, and the configuration list or Income in every theme. Also not checked: a real window switch and a real file drag (window return was exercised by script and file drag was only read), the replayed click during a live transition (the pane was hidden, so the click may have landed after the transition; it left no stuck canvas), the fan pulse in its still state, and whether a tap below a collapsing panel on a phone is lost, which is the same cause as the Run button and remains open because it needs a real device. The kanban cards remain mouse and touch only, as before; they have no keyboard path, while the configuration list keeps its arrow keys.

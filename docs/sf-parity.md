# Story Forge workspace parity

Authority: decisions [0006](decisions/0006-full-story-forge-workspace.md), [0008](decisions/0008-story-forge-production-logic.md) and [0010](decisions/0010-story-forge-timeline-monitor-and-chat.md), and `UI-RULES.md`. Decision 0010 replaces the affected copied interactions and script scope; the production model from 0008 remains in force.

## 2026-10-30 production revision

Decision 0008 deliberately replaces the prototype's film data. Prototype AST equality is no longer an acceptance oracle for the model or any view derived from it. The old v5 inventory and its observations are historical, not evidence for this revision. Its exact original text is retained at `.claude/verification-story-forge/original-sf-parity.md` (repository root-relative).

The workspace remains in `templates/html/src/02-work.html`, `templates/html/src/sf.js` and `core/styles/sf.css`. The shared UX layer owns hover, focus and the attention lock. Story Forge Create applies the narrow watching/dimming exceptions recorded in `UI-RULES.md`; this is not a second attention layer or an exception for other samples. No iframe, backend generation, new theme or asset generation is introduced.

## 2026-10-31 interaction revision

Decision 0010 is the current interaction contract. The owner ruled that watching the monitor is a deliberate mode change, not an ordinary hover preview. The timeline keeps its geometry while the monitor grows; chat focus alone neither dims the browser nor retires the timeline. The old centred hero, per-level script-only view and browser player are superseded. The rows below retain valid production facts and identify the replacements, not claims of source-byte parity or completed browser verification.

### Interaction contract to copy

| Area / decision 0010 | Required behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Slot geometry and film surface (1–5) | Every video shot owns 110×62px, independent of duration. A unit occupies `members × 110px`, with square internal joins, rounded outer corners and a 1px side inset. Script text and label share one full-height dark block. Strip, lane heads and magnifier use the `strip-*` tokens on black in all six themes. No scroll snap or scroll-driven selection remains. Strip edge fades are 24px, with matching end pads: 24px for video, lane gutter + 12px for lanes. |
| Time and transport (6–8) | `playback.time` is the source of truth; shot and unit selection derive from it. Video maps each shot's duration into its fixed slot; lanes use `time × PX_PER_SEC`. Track changes keep time. Pointer press seeks, captured drag follows, and the outer 72px edge bands auto-scroll proportionally up to 14px per frame. Release stops the drag; movement under 4px also picks material without re-centring. The 2px playhead and head use `strip-head`. Toolbar play/pause and Space while the timeline has focus advance real time, keep the playhead in the middle 70% and stop at the cut's end. |
| Pointer magnifier (9) | Only the video track magnifies, only while neither playing nor dragging. Scale the pointed block by `100/62` around its own centre, clamp horizontally to the wrap, keep its top constant and its caption on one line. Oversized Film/Act blocks become a wrap-width loupe whose content follows the pointer. Adjacent blocks switch directly with no fade cycle; leaving the strip hides it. |
| Toolbar and labels (10–11) | One reserved-height `1fr auto 1fr` row holds layer, transport/cut/track and scope groups with aligned bottoms and a fixed gap above the strip. Tools fade only: no position transform, delay or negative overlap. Narrow containers wrap to two rows, centre group first. The cut menu anchors to the centre group. Intro, controls, scope flash and English labels use the same Script / Storyboard / Film and Full film / Act / Scene / Beat / Shot vocabulary. |
| Monitor and script (12–13) | Storyboard and Film always have a non-scrolling 16:9 monitor, with current/total timecode, shot name and description, duration and an honest timed-still note. Storyboard follows each shot's panel; Film follows its still. Shot 12 is black in both. Scrubbing only changes the picture background and metadata, never rebuilds the monitor. Below it, Shot Film shows takes, higher scopes show unit grids, and a picked clip shows its detail. Secondary content refreshes only on unit change, debounced 160ms, never while watching. Script has no monitor: the whole active cut stays visible, highlights follow current coverage using `--mark` or `--sel`, and scrolling keeps that coverage in view. |
| Watching and restoration (14) | `.sf.is-watching` follows timeline hover attention or keyboard focus, dragging or playback; hover exit has 200ms grace. On touch only playback and drags watch. The monitor grows to the largest fitting 16:9 with its info line, secondary height becomes zero, the middle width cap lifts, the script column widens and the side panel gives way. Browser and monitor stay undimmed. Remove only the watching class to restore the prior panel, `activeSel`, secondary scroll and picked block; size transitions use `--ease` and stay monotonic. Monitor hover does not hold this state. |
| Lanes, overflow and semantic colour (15–18) | Rows are 24px: voice/sound/subtitles have two rows each; music/graphics one. Compute each lane and its head height from the same data; clip selection never grows a lane. Story Forge uses `lh-voice` and matching scoped names, never the shared `.voice` card class. Labels sit in a black gutter outside the fade, category dividers use `strip-line`, alternate categories have a faint band and background footage is at .5 opacity. Clip text is vertically centred and fades only on measured overflow. The five `trk-*` category colours match label text, dot and clips in every theme; each reaches 4.5:1 on black, and `trk-on` reaches 4.5:1 on each. |
| Cinema preview (19) | Media uses `rgba(8,8,10,.84)` with `blur(3px)` in every theme, with a light caption and close button. A 16:9 picture fits within 80% of workspace width and 72% of height, reserves the chat bar, exceeds the resting monitor and is not full-screen. Text-document previews retain `--scrim`. |
| Chat focus and mentions (20–21) | Focus changes only bar width, shadow and send visibility; history stays closed, browser fades/masks stay unchanged, and no 230px scroll padding is added. The bar grows in normal flow: more text pushes the timeline upward; one line changes nothing. The wrapper has no pointer events; only visible bar/history/toggle bodies do. Mentions are any number of inline noneditable chips at the saved caret. Focused picks commit chips without duplicates; unfocused picks replace one tentative chip, deselection removes it and focus commits it. `@` filters shots, characters and locations at the caret; Enter/click inserts, Esc closes. Native Backspace removes a chip atomically; sending serializes it as `@name`. The input masks only real overflow, never complete chips. |
| History states (22) | Closed, peek and pinned are distinct. A chevron appears only when focused or pinned in a permanent gap of about 26px above the bar. Hover peeks in a glass overlay over the timeline; leaving button and history closes after 200ms. Click pins history in normal flow, collapses the timeline and gives the browser the remaining height; click again restores it. Pinned survives blur. Sending while closed opens peek; chat blur closes an unpinned peek. |

## Core ownership after the template split

Decision0010 design values belong to `core/styles/sf.css`. The HTML Story Forge reads the `--sf-*` custom properties at run time; shot data and application state stay in `templates/html/src/sf.js`. Runtime values cover slot/lane geometry, drag and watching timing, magnifier scale, mention placement, waveform rendering and canvas layout. The existing shared `data-ph` language binding supplies the chat placeholder. The Create no-dim exception explicitly overrides the shared important dim rule only in this workspace.

Below a 560px Story Forge container, the toolbar uses three rows (centre, layer, scope). The two-row English selectors measured407px within a362px phone workspace; a third row keeps every label and target visible. Wider layouts retain the two-row/desktop arrangement. This is the narrow purpose-preserving departure from decision10, not a shared toolbar rule.

## Verification and limits

Decision 0010 requires A1–A15 with real pointer input at 1440×900 in Pure and Neon, plus the specified six-theme strip, lane-colour and media-preview checks. Per-animation-frame geometry measurements and visual review are both required: a passing number does not excuse hidden or broken material. New source/data checks, browser observations, screenshots and failures belong under `.claude/verification-story-forge/0009/`; the delivery report is the evidence index. This document records the contract, not an independent claim that those checks passed. A completed build or a passing static assertion is not behavioral proof. Historical E2–E9 v5 logs and earlier 0008 observations do not establish the changed interactions.

The prototype's chat-focused timeline retirement and its resulting inability to click a frame during focus are no longer the contract. Focus now leaves the timeline available for caret-position material picks; only pinned history hides it. Mock chat streams local text. Timed still playback is not motion-video playback, framing studies are not new generated takes, and audio cues are records rather than rendered audio. Hardware touch, non-Chromium engines, real AI generation and rendered audio remain unverified unless the current evidence explicitly establishes them.
